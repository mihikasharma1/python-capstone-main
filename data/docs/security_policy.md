# Security Policy

All employees must complete security awareness training within 30 days of
hire and annually thereafter. Multi-factor authentication is required for
all systems that touch customer data. Laptops must have full-disk encryption
enabled before they leave IT provisioning.

Incidents (lost devices, phishing clicks, suspected breaches) must be
reported to security@company.example within 1 hour of discovery. The
security team triages within 4 business hours and files a written summary
within 5 business days.

Access to production databases requires manager approval and is reviewed
quarterly. Contractors get read-only access by default and it expires
automatically after 90 days unless renewed.
