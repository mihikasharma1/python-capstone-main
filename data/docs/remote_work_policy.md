# Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to 4 days per week; at least 1 day per week
should be spent at a company office or approved coworking space for teams
that have one within reasonable commuting distance. Fully remote roles
(no office within 50 miles) are exempt from the in-office day and are
reviewed case by case by HR.

Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM in the employee's local
time zone, during which they should be reachable on chat and available for
meetings. Outside of core hours, employees can set their own schedule as
long as deliverables land on time.

Company equipment (laptop, monitor, peripherals) is provided for remote
setups; a one-time $300 home office stipend is available in the first 90
days of employment or role transition to remote. Expense claims for home
office equipment must be submitted within 60 days of purchase with a
receipt.

Employees working remotely from a different country for more than 15
consecutive days must notify HR in advance for tax and compliance reasons.
Approval is not guaranteed and depends on the destination country's
regulations. Working remotely from a different country without notifying
HR is treated as a policy violation, not just an oversight.
