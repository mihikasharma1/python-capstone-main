# Onboarding Guide — Meridian Retail

New hires receive their laptop and account credentials on day 1, provisioned
by IT the business day before their start date. Week 1 focuses on
orientation: company overview, security awareness training, tool access
verification, and a 1:1 with the new hire's manager to set 30/60/90-day
goals. New hires are paired with a buddy from their team for their first 2
weeks.

By the end of week 2, new hires should have completed all required
compliance training modules and made at least one small contribution to get
familiar with the code review process end to end.

**Customer activation target:** on the commercial side, Meridian Retail's
customer success team targets a 70% activation rate — meaning 70% of newly
signed-up customers should reach "active, repeat-purchase" status within 90
days of signup. This is tracked internally by the customer success
analytics team through a separate onboarding funnel dashboard, not through
the core sales database.

The 30-day and 90-day employee check-ins are a written self-assessment plus
a manager conversation covering ramp-up progress, blockers, and goals for
the next period.
