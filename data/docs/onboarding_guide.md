# Onboarding Guide

New hires receive their laptop and account credentials on day 1, provisioned
by IT the business day before their start date. Accounts are created from
the HR system's start-date record, so any last-minute start-date changes
should be flagged to HR at least 3 business days in advance to avoid a
delayed provisioning.

Week 1 focuses on orientation: company overview, security awareness
training (see the Security Policy), tool access verification, and a
1:1 with the new hire's manager to set 30/60/90-day goals. New hires are
paired with a buddy from their team for their first 2 weeks, someone other
than their manager, to ask day-to-day questions.

By the end of week 2, new hires should have completed all required
compliance training modules and made at least one small contribution
(a documentation fix, a small bug fix, or equivalent) to get familiar with
the code review process end to end.

The 30-day check-in is a written self-assessment plus a manager conversation
covering ramp-up progress and any blockers. The 90-day check-in determines
whether the new hire's initial goals were met and sets goals for the next
quarter. HR sends automated reminders for both check-ins, but it's the
manager's responsibility to actually schedule and hold them.
