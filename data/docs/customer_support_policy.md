# Customer Support Policy — Meridian Retail

Support tickets are triaged into three severities. Severity 1 (service down,
data loss risk) must receive a first response within 30 minutes and a status
update every hour until resolved. Severity 2 (major feature broken, no
workaround) requires a first response within 4 business hours. Severity 3
(minor issue, question, or feature request) requires a first response
within 1 business day.

All customer-facing responses must go through the shared support inbox, not
personal email. When an issue requires an engineering fix, the support agent
opens a linked engineering ticket and keeps the customer updated at least
every 2 business days until it ships.

Refunds and credits under $100 can be approved directly by a support agent.
Refunds between $100 and $1,000 require a team lead's sign-off. Anything
above $1,000 goes to the finance team for approval.

**Churn target:** Meridian Retail's company-wide target is to keep quarterly
customer churn below 10%. Any individual region exceeding 15% churn in a
quarter is automatically escalated to the regional customer success lead for
a root-cause review, and a remediation plan is due within 2 weeks of
escalation. Regions currently covered: North America, EMEA, APAC, and LATAM.

Complaints escalated by a customer twice on the same issue are automatically
flagged to a team lead for review, regardless of severity.
