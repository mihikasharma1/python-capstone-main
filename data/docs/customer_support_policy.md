# Customer Support Policy

Support tickets are triaged into three severities. Severity 1 (service
down, data loss risk) must receive a first response within 30 minutes and a
status update every hour until resolved. Severity 2 (major feature broken,
no workaround) requires a first response within 4 business hours. Severity
3 (minor issue, question, or feature request) requires a first response
within 1 business day.

All customer-facing responses must go through the shared support inbox, not
personal email, so that ownership is visible if someone is out. When an
issue requires an engineering fix, the support agent opens a linked
engineering ticket and keeps the customer updated at least every 2 business
days until it ships.

Refunds and credits under $100 can be approved directly by a support agent.
Refunds between $100 and $1,000 require a team lead's sign-off. Anything
above $1,000 goes to the finance team for approval. Every refund, regardless
of amount, must be logged with a reason code for quarterly reporting.

Complaints escalated by a customer twice on the same issue are automatically
flagged to a team lead for review, regardless of severity. Support agents
are expected to close the loop with the customer in plain language, avoiding
internal jargon like ticket IDs or system names unless the customer asks.
